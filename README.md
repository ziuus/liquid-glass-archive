# 🗃️ Liquid Glass Design Archive & Lab

Historical design research, early optical mockups, launch kits, and promotional materials for the **[Liquid Glass Engine](https://github.com/ziuus/liquid-glass)** and **[Atlantis Desktop](https://github.com/ziuus/atlantis)**.

---

## 📂 Repository Contents

### 1. `docs/` — Research & Articles
- **`FLUID_DYNAMICS_RESEARCH.md`**: Physical Snell refraction and Cauchy chromatic dispersion equations.
- **`LIQUID_GLASS_ANALYSIS.md`**: Optical shader study comparing frosted glassmorphism against physical liquid lensing.
- **`ARTICLE_DEVTO_HASHNODE.md`**: Technical writeups and long-form publications.
- **`PROMOTIONAL.md` & `TWITTER_THREAD.md`**: Social media showcase kits and promotional drafts.
- **`showcase.html`**: Web-based optical interactive preview prototype.

### 2. `docs/references/` — Visual Inspiration & References
- Reference UI mockups, camera controls, media capsules, and dynamic island layout studies.

### 3. `assets/` — Early Mockups & Photo Dumps
- Hardware camera captures, test render dumps, and experimental WGSL shaders (`row_ink.wgsl`).

## 🔗 Related Projects
